Experience

Undergrad research assistant Aug. 2025 — Nov. 2025

I was assigned to design and prototype two types of energy harvesting circuits: thermal and vibration-based. I developed both prototypes using a thermoelectric generator, a piezoelectric energy harvester, and using suitable boost converter integrated circuits. After assembling and testing the circuits on a protoboard using an oscilloscope, I designed a custom two-layer PCB in Altium Designer. Once manufactured, I assembled and tested the PCB, confirming that it worked as expected.

The project was later extended as part of a research project focused on increasing the reading range of RFID tags. I connected the thermal energy harvesting circuit to an RFID tag and demonstrated that harvesting energy could increase its reading distance. A solar energy harvesting circuit was also tested and produced similar results.

Following the measurements, I co-authored a research paper that was accepted by IEEE. The work was also presented at the Horizon of Innovations event, where I had the opportunity to discuss our findings.
